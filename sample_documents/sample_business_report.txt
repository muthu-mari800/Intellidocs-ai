Quarterly Business Report - Q3 FY2025
Orion Retail Pvt Ltd
Prepared by the Finance and Strategy Team
Reporting period: 1 July 2025 to 30 September 2025

Executive summary
Orion Retail delivered a strong third quarter. Revenue reached $48.2 million, up 12.5% year-over-year,
driven by growth in online sales. Operating margin improved to 14.3%.

Key figures
Revenue: $48.2 million
Operating margin: 14.3%
Net profit: $5.6 million
New customers: 12,400
Customer churn: 3.1%
Market share: 8.7%

Findings
Online sales grew faster than store sales and now represent 41% of revenue.
Customer churn fell compared with the previous quarter after the loyalty programme launch.
Supply chain delays in the Chennai warehouse increased logistics costs.

Risks
Rising logistics costs and dependence on two major suppliers, Vertex Foods and Lumen Packaging.

Recommendations
Expand the loyalty programme, diversify suppliers and invest in warehouse automation.
The board will review the forecast on 15 October 2025.
