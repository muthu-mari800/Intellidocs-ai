Database Management Systems - Syllabus
Course code: CS301    Credits: 4    Semester: V
Department of Computer Science, Riverside University
Instructor: Dr. Anitha Rao
Prerequisite: Data Structures (CS201)

Course objectives
To introduce the fundamentals of relational databases, SQL, normalization and transaction management.

Learning outcomes
Students will be able to design an ER model, write SQL queries, normalize relations up to BCNF
and explain ACID properties and concurrency control.

Units
Unit I: Introduction to databases and ER modelling
Unit II: Relational model and SQL
Unit III: Normalization and functional dependencies
Unit IV: Transactions and concurrency control
Unit V: Indexing and query processing

Grading
Internal assessment 40 percent, final examination 60 percent. Attendance of at least 75 percent is required.

Weekly schedule and important dates
Classes begin: 4 August 2025
Mid-term examination: 15 October 2025
Final examination: 10 December 2025

Textbook: Database System Concepts by Silberschatz, Korth and Sudarshan.
Reference books: Fundamentals of Database Systems by Elmasri and Navathe.
